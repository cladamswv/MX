# Background assets — Arcade 3.1

The active course backdrops in 3.1 are original procedural 3D assets generated for this project, not flat AI images.

Active assets:
- `models/props/background_pine_ridge_mp.glb`
- `models/props/background_red_mesa_mp.glb`
- `models/props/background_quarry_mp.glb`

They are medium-poly GLBs with UV coordinates and embedded PBR textures. Their editable texture maps are stored in `textures/backgrounds/`.

Free asset libraries researched for future prop expansion include Kenney Nature Kit and Quaternius nature packs, but these medium-poly background GLBs are original to DIRTLINE MX and do not require third-party attribution.
